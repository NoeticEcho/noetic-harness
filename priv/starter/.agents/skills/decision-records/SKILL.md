# Decision records

A decision record says:

1. **Context**: what forced the decision, and the constraints.
2. **Options**: each one seriously considered, with what it would have cost.
3. **Decision**: the choice, in one sentence.
4. **Consequences**: what becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what would
   make us revisit it.

A record is never deleted. A decision that is replaced is marked superseded and
points at its successor, so the reasoning stays readable.

Write one when a change alters a boundary, a public interface, or where a kind
of data lives.
