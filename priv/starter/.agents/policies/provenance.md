# Provenance

Every artifact records where it came from: the task, the agent, the workflow
and step, the inputs it read and the evidence it rests on. An artifact without
provenance can be believed, but not evaluated.

Artifacts produced by tools outside the repository, such as a scanner's report
or a page read in a browser, record the tool, its version where known, and when
it ran.

Data brought in from outside records its source and the terms it may be used
under, before it is used.
